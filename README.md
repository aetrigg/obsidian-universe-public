## ✨ About

After multiple requests from the community, I created this public version for others to peruse. I'll do my best to keep it up-to-date with my main universe, as it's still under construction and constantly evolving!

> [!NOTE] 
> This vault started as a personal life management experiment and turned into an entire cosmic system ✨

---

## ✨ Features

- 🌠 **Cosmic Metaphor System** — Notes are stars, tags are constellations, folders are nebulae, and projects are galaxies
- 📓 **Digital Hobonichi** — Daily/weekly/monthly planning system based on the analog Hobonichi planner
- ✅ **Mission Management** — Task system with 10 categorized sub-INBOXes across life areas
- 📊 **Observatories** — Custom dashboards for activity tracking, finances, signal frequency scanning, and more
- 🎨 **Color-Coded Organization** — Unified color language across tags, graph view, and folder regions
- ✍️ **Creative Writing Galaxy** — Series bible, drafts, and world-building for the _5th of June_ boy band novel series
- 🖼️ **ColoringtheCosmos** — Galaxy dedicated to Instagram/TikTok cozy coloring content (@coloringthecosmos)

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Clone the repository:
    
    ```
    git clone https://github.com/aetrigg/obsidian-universe-public.git
    ```
    
    Or download as ZIP.
2. Open [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/).
3. Select **"Open folder as vault"** and choose the downloaded folder.
4. Open **HITCHHIKER'S GUIDE.md** for a full guided tour of the vault's cosmic terminology, color system, folder structure, and plugins.

> [!WARNING] 
> This vault is optimized for **reading view** — all views are designed with reading mode in mind.

> [!TIP] 
> New to the universe? Start with **MISSION CONTROL.md** (the home page) and explore from there. The **NORTH STAR.md** sidebar cheat sheet is also a great quick reference.

```
.
├── 5OJ/                    — Boy band novel series galaxy (W.I.P.)

│   ├── Drafts & Revisions/     — Novel drafts and works in progress
│   └── Note Imports/            — Notes from OneNote, Google Docs, ChatGPT
├── COLORINGTHECOSMOS/      — Instagram/TikTok cozy coloring content galaxy
│   └── Color Palettes/         — Ohuhu marker color combos
├── Digital Hobonichi/      — Daily/weekly/monthly planning galaxy
│   └── [Year]/
│       ├── [Month]/             — Daily stars
│       ├── Monthly Overview/    — Monthly summaries with visualizations
│       └── Weekly Overview/     — Weekly planning canvases
├── INBOX/                  — Mission staging and organization nebula
│   ├── 00_MAIN.md               — Aggregated view of all open missions
│   ├── 01_PERSONAL.md           — Personal life missions
│   ├── 02_CHORES.md             — Household missions
│   ├── 03_FINANCES.md           — Financial missions
│   ├── 04_WRITING.md            — Writing projects
│   ├── 05_COLORING.md           — CTC content missions
│   ├── 06_FAMILY-FRIENDS.md     — Social and family missions
│   ├── 07_RECURRING.md          — Recurring missions and events
│   ├── 08_UNIVERSE.md           — Vault development missions
│   └── 99_UNSORTED.md           — QuickAdd landing zone
├── OBSERVATORIES/          — Dashboards and data views
│   ├── Finances/                — Bills, expenses, and financial analytics
│   ├── Activity Observatory.md  — Generated using the Daily Activity plugin
│   ├── Coloring Book Observatory.base
│   └── Frequency Scanner.md     — Quickly view previous signals
├── SYSTEM/                 — Vault infrastructure and utilities
│   ├── Launchpads/              — Quick navigation cards
│   ├── _ai/                     — Agent documentation and prompts
│   ├── _data/                   — Data storage (bills, shows, etc.)
│   ├── _media/                  — Images, gifs, banners
│   ├── _scripts/                — Templater scripts, JS, Datacore components
│   ├── _snippets/               — CSS snippets and HTML widgets
│   └── _templates/              — Templates for dailies, weeklies, monthlies
├── THE BLACK HOLE/         — Archive for old and inactive stars
├── MISSION CONTROL.md      — Home page and cosmic landing pad
├── HITCHHIKER'S GUIDE.md  — Full guided tour and reference guide
└── NORTH STAR.md           — Quick-reference sidebar cheat sheet
```

## ⚠️ Disclaimers

> [!CAUTION] 
> **This is a personal system, not a starter template.** It was built for my own workflow and may not suit everyone out of the box. Use it as inspiration, not a drop-in replacement.

- **Desktop only** — Many features and custom components were designed for desktop and may not work on mobile.
- **Reading view optimized** — All views are designed for reading mode over editing mode.
- **AI-assisted** — Much of this vault was shaped with AI as co-pilot (alongside my own web design background).
- **Visual content** — Photos, gifs, and banners used throughout are not mine. All copyright belongs to their respective owners.
- **Living system** — Constellations shift, nebulae expand, and plugins update. I can't guarantee everything will work perfectly at all times.
- **Use at your own risk** — I cannot be held responsible for any stardust lost along the way. Keep your own vault backed up!

> [!IMPORTANT] 
> If you notice something broken or unclear, feel free to [open an issue](https://github.com/aetrigg/obsidian-universe-public/issues) — this is my first public repo, so tips and patience are appreciated!
